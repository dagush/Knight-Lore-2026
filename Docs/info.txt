Coords:
My student detected that the exact expressions to go from low-resolution integer coordinates, where objects are initialised in memory, are translated to high-resolution coordinates are:
    X_h = X_L * 16 + X_l * 8 + 72
    Y_h = Y_L * 16 + Y_l * 8 + 72
    Z_h = Z_L * 12 + Z_l * 4 + ScreenZ
Where X_L, Y_L and Z_L are the low resolution coordinates, the _l is information related to the sprites and allow to work with self-moving objects, and ScreenZ is always 128.

Main Character:
    The head location information is located at 5C20h, 5C2Ah and 5C2Bh for X, Y and Z.
    The body location information is at         5C09h, 5C0Ah and 5C0Bh.
    Positions 5C41h and 5C45h indicate the head and body sprite codes.
    Positions 5C0Fh and 5C2Fh contain the mirror flags for orientation.

Current Room
     id: 5C10h
     color: 5BADh
     size: 5BABh, 5BACh and 5BAEh (X, Y, Z)
     Walls: use static information

Working memory: between 5BA0h and 6107h

Note: guard head: 0x96: west, 0x97: east
      guard body: 0x90~0x95: west, 0x98~0x9D: east
      portcullis: 0x08~0x09 west/east
      ball: 0xB2~0xB3
      ghost: 0x50~0x52

items/charms: During the game, the player has to collect and bring to the mage a number of items,
       of 8 different types. I have found this page:
       https://www.porotal.org/knightlore/
       which describes, from a player's perspective, where they can be found (and in
       which order they must be retrieved).
       In this document:
       https://www.icemark.com/dataformats/knightlore/index.html
       it seems they are stored starting at 0x6ff2 (until 0x7111), in groups of 9 bytes.
       The screen (room?) where they are located is coded in offset 04, their current position
       is in 05, 06 and 07.
       -> The items in the table 0x6ff2 are updated once the player LEAVES the room leaving the
       item behind, not while the player is in the room toying around.
item codes:
       0x60: diamond/gem
       0x61: poison
       0x62: shoe/boot
       0x63: chalice/vase
       0x64: cup
       0x65: bottle
       0x66: ball/crystal ball
       0x67: life
item locations in working memory (only 2):
1st:   0x5C48 -> sprite/object ID 1
       0x5C49 ~ 0x5C4B -> XYZ
       0x5C50 -> room ID
2nd:   0x5C68 -> sprite/object ID 2
       0x5C69 ~ 0x5C6B -> X, Y, Z
       0x5C70 -> room ID

wizard: 0x9F and 0x9E -> head
